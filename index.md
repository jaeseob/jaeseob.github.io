    <h2>Research Interests</h2>

    <p class="section-description">
      I explore how visual representations are shaped
      by interactions across time, relationships between
      items, and the structure of representational space.
    </p>

    <div class="research-list">

      <article class="research-row">

          </div>

          <h3>
            History Effects<br>
            in Visual Cognition
          </h3>
        </div>

        <div class="research-row-content">

          <p class="question">
            How do past experiences and decisions
            shape current visual cognition?
          </p>

          <p>
            I investigate serial dependence and
            history-dependent biases, focusing on how
            previous decisions, contextual information,
            and task relevance influence visual perception
            and working memory.
          </p>

        </div>
      </article>


      <!-- 02: RELATIONAL STRUCTURE -->
      <article class="research-row">

        <div class="research-row-heading">
          <div class="card-number">

          </div>

          <h3>
            Relational Structure<br>
            in Working Memory
          </h3>
        </div>

        <div class="research-row-content">

          <p class="question">
            How do relationships between remembered
            items constrain individual representations?
          </p>

          <p>
            I study interactions between visual memory
            contents, examining how relational structure
            constrains individual memory estimates
            and shapes the organization of information
            in working memory.
          </p>

        </div>
      </article>
