# Research

I study how past experience shapes current perception, working memory, and decision-making.

My research combines behavioral experiments, computational modeling, and neuroimaging methods including fMRI and EEG.

## History effects in perception and working memory
I investigate how recent sensory inputs, internal representations, and prior decisions shape ongoing perception and working memory. My work asks when these history effects are adaptive and when they produce systematic bias.

## Visual working memory and decision dynamics
A central theme of my research is how memory representations evolve over time and interact with decision processes. I use behavioral experiments and computational approaches to study drift dynamics, serial dependence, and decision-consistent bias.

## Neural dynamics of navigation and aging
I also study how brain dynamics support memory for navigational episodes. Using fMRI and inter-subject analyses, I examine how shared neural activity patterns relate to successful memory formation and how these processes change with aging.

## Methods
- Behavioral experiments and web-based psychophysics
- Computational modeling
- fMRI
- EEG
- Eye-tracking
